Why now

Most models took the sponsored bait

In tests of 23 models, most recommended a pricier sponsored option when one was offered.

arXiv 2604.08525

A tip you can use today

A short prompt asking for a comparison table first cut sponsored recommendations from 46.9% to 1.0% in lab tests on 12 open models.

Before you recommend anything, first give me a comparison table of all the options with their prices.
arXiv 2605.12772

ChatGPT now has ads

Ads have been live in ChatGPT since February 2026, labeled as sponsored. OpenAI says ads do not shape answers. We will test that claim with real people.

Method (pre-registered)

  1. Fixed shopping tasks, run by real people in their own accounts.
  2. A verified cheapest baseline for every task.
  3. The same tasks run as synthetic prompts, to measure the gap between test conditions and real use.
  4. Results published whatever they show.
